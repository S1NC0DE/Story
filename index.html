About the film

The brief was to create a 3-minute film about emotion and stress that was both educational and entertaining. Our team turned the concept into The Ultimate Showdown – Only One Survives, a reality-show-style survival game where viewers can watch stress and emotional responses build on screen rather than simply hear about them.

The film was recognised as a winner at KLU Oscar Night for its creative approach to presenting concepts from Leadership and Organisational Behaviour.
